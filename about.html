What is Comparator?

This tool suite is designed for developers and writers who need precise, noise-free text analysis. The primary feature is a Longest Common Subsequence (LCS) based difference engine that highlights exact insertions and deletions between two bodies of text. It operates entirely client-side, ensuring your data never leaves your browser.
